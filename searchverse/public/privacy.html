Searchverse — Privacy policy

Searchverse (GA & GSC Connectors) is an internal SEO reporting tool. This page explains what it does with Google data.

What we access

  • Sign-in: your name, email address and profile picture, to identify you.
  • Google Search Console and Google Analytics 4 (read-only): only for properties you choose to add to a project, to build SEO reports.
  • Gmail “send email” (optional, admins only): to send invite emails from the admin's own mailbox. Searchverse cannot read any email.

How we use it

Data is used only to show reports, scores and recommendations inside Searchverse to the people you give access to. It is never sold, shared with advertisers or used for anything else. Google user data is handled in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage & deletion

Reports are stored in Cloudflare databases. Google access tokens are stored encrypted. You can disconnect a Google account at any time in Searchverse settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions; admins can delete projects and runs, which removes their data.

AI

Summarised report data may be sent to Google Gemini or Cloudflare Workers AI to generate recommendations. No sign-in or account credentials are sent.

Contact

Questions: contact the Searchverse administrator who invited you.
